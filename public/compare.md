# Uno Blueprint and journey management tools

Uno Blueprint is an open-source context-engineering toolkit: it turns a team's scattered context into service blueprints that people and AI agents work from.

## How each tool describes itself

- [TheyDo](https://www.theydo.com): "The Experience Context Platform", the structure that turns scattered data into customer context every team can rely on and AI agents can reason over.
- [Smaply](https://www.smaply.com): "Customer journey management that turns evidence into action", bringing research, journeys, and metrics into one system.
- [Cemantica](https://www.cemantica.com): "Customer Journey Management that drives business value with AI technology and CX consulting."
- [JourneyTrack](https://www.journeytrack.io): "Decision intelligence for customer journeys."

## What Uno Blueprint does

- **Open source, MIT license.** Change anything, from the canvas to the skills, to fit your team.
- **The whole service.** Each blueprint lays out the customer's steps and the frontstage, backstage, and support work behind them. Each cell carries an owner, a status, its dependencies, and its sources.
- **Your agent, your database.** The skills run in Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex. The blueprint lives in a database you own.
- **Checked, not just drawn.** Every import passes a validator before it lands, and an audit lists gaps, conflicts, and stale sources.
- **Changes traced first.** A what-if follows what a change would touch before anyone makes it.

## Using them together

A journey management tool runs a customer experience program across many journeys. Uno Blueprint keeps a shared map of how your services run, for your team and your agents. A team can keep both, and bring an existing board or spreadsheet into Uno Blueprint.
